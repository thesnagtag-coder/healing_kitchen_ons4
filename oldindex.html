How the numbers are worked out

  • Nutrition is calculated per serving from USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy) values per 100 g (3.5 oz) of each ingredient. Values are estimates - brands and exact sizes vary.
  • Carbs are total carbohydrate including fibre. Net carbs = total carbs minus fibre. Many UK and EU labels already list carbs without fibre, so compare like with like.
  • Kitchen measures: 1 tsp = 5 ml, 1 tbsp = 15 ml, 1 cup = 240 ml. Ounces rounded to the nearest 1/2 oz.
  • Safe cooking temperatures follow USDA FSIS minimums: poultry 74°C (165°F), minced beef 71°C (160°F), whole pork cuts 63°C (145°F) plus a 3 min rest, fish and shellfish 63°C (145°F), egg dishes 71°C (160°F). Chill leftovers within 2 hours, fridge at 5°C (41°F) or below.
  • Very Low Carb recipes have 20 g net carbs or less per serving. Dairy-Free recipes contain no milk, yogurt, cheese, cream or butter (eggs are used in some). Always check labels on bread and chocolate, as some contain milk.
  • Water, stock, salt, pepper and dried herbs under 1 g are not counted in the nutrition. Low-salt stock adds roughly 1-2 g carbs per serving.
  • Air fryer settings are for the Ninja Double Stack XL (SL400), based on the Ninja SL400UK Quick Start Guide charts. Always insert the crisper plate: Upper position for proteins (better browning), Lower position for vegetables. Shake vegetables at least 3 times. No preheat needed. Times are a guide. Our 2-serving loads are smaller than the chart loads, so each panel says when to start checking - always confirm doneness with a food thermometer. °F is rounded to the nearest 5°F setting.
  • These recipes support healthy eating but are not medical advice. If you manage diabetes with insulin or medication, check carb portions with your doctor or dietitian.
